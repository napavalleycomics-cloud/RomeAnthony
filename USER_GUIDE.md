# 🍽️ Otto Fridge - Easy User Guide

## Welcome!

Otto Fridge helps you remember what food is in your fridge.

Never buy the same thing twice. Never let food go bad.

---

## 📖 How to Use Otto Fridge

### 1️⃣ Adding Food to Your Fridge

**When you buy food at the store:**

1. Open the app
2. Tap **"Add Items"** at the bottom of the screen
3. Look for your food:
   - **Browse** the categories (Vegetables, Fruits, Meat, etc.)
   - **Search** by typing the food name
4. Tap the food you bought
5. See a **green checkmark** ✓ (this means it was added!)
6. Keep adding more food
7. When done, tap **"View Fridge"**

**Example:**
- You bought tomatoes, bread, and milk
- Tap each one in the app
- They're now in your digital fridge!

---

### 2️⃣ Seeing What's in Your Fridge

**On the Home page:**

- **Top of screen** = All your food items
  - Scroll left and right to see everything
  - Each item shows its picture

- **Visual fridge** = See your food organized like a real fridge
  - Freezer items on top
  - Fresh food in middle
  - Vegetables at bottom

- **Colors mean something:**
  - 🟢 **Green border** = Food is fresh
  - 🟡 **Yellow border** = Food expires soon (eat it this week!)
  - 🔴 **Red border** = Food expired (throw it away)

---

### 3️⃣ Removing Food

**When you eat something or throw it away:**

1. Find the food at the top of the Home page
2. Look for the **X button** (red circle with X)
3. Tap the **X**
4. The food is removed!
5. Your phone will vibrate (you'll feel it)

**Note:** If you have 2 tomatoes and remove 1, you'll still have 1 tomato left.

---

### 4️⃣ Getting Recipe Ideas

**Don't know what to cook? Let Otto help!**

1. Tap **"Recipes"** at the bottom
2. See recipes you can make RIGHT NOW
   - These use food you already have
   - They're marked with ✨ (sparkles)
3. Tap any recipe to see:
   - What ingredients you need
   - Step-by-step instructions
   - How long it takes

**Example:**
- You have eggs, tomatoes, and bread
- Otto suggests: "Tomato & Egg Scramble"
- Tap it to see how to make it!

---

### 5️⃣ Understanding Reminders

**Otto will remind you when food is expiring:**

- 📢 You'll get a message
- 📳 Your phone will vibrate
- 🔔 The message says which food expires soon

**Example:**
"Milk expires in 2 days"

This means: **Use your milk soon!**

---

## ⚙️ Settings

**Tap the gear icon ⚙️ on the Home page to change settings:**

### Reminders
- **Turn on/off** notifications
- **Change reminder days** (how many days before expiry to remind you)
  - Options: 1, 2, 3, 5, or 7 days

### Feedback
- **Sound** - Hear beeps when you tap buttons
- **Vibration** - Feel your phone vibrate when you tap
- **Voice** - Hear the app read things out loud

### Help
- **Restart Tutorial** - See the welcome guide again

### Data
- **Clear Fridge** - Remove all food from the app
  - ⚠️ **Warning:** This deletes everything!
  - Only use this if you want to start over

## 💡 Tips for Success

### Daily Habits
✅ **Add food right away** when you get home from the store
✅ **Check your fridge** in the app before going shopping
✅ **Remove items** as soon as you use them
✅ **Look at recipes** when you don't know what to cook

### Understanding Colors

| Color | What It Means | What To Do |
|-------|---------------|------------|
| 🟢 Green | Food is fresh | Enjoy anytime! |
| 🟡 Yellow | Expires in 1-2 days | Eat it soon! |
| 🔴 Red | Expired | Throw it away |

### Common Questions

**Q: What if I can't find my food in the list?**
- Try searching for it
- Look in different categories
- Use a similar item (example: use "chicken" for all types of chicken)

**Q: What if I added something by mistake?**
- Just tap the **X button** to remove it
- Don't worry, you can always add it back!

**Q: How do I know when my food expires?**
- Look at the color border around each item
- Check the "Expiring Soon" alert on the Home page
- The app calculates it automatically!

**Q: Can I use this with my caregiver or family?**
- Yes! Show them your fridge
- They can help you add or remove items
- You can plan meals together using the recipes
